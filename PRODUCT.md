# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Developers, AI engineers, and knowledge workers setting up retrieval-augmented generation pipelines for their data. They are technical, efficiency-minded, and looking for reliable tools.

## Product Purpose
RecRAG makes it easy to ingest, vectorize, and query enterprise knowledge. The success of the app relies on establishing trust, transparency, and high performance in document processing.

## Brand Personality
Professional, precise, technical, clean.

## Anti-references
- No overly playful illustrations or "whimsical" doodles.
- No heavy glassmorphism or busy gradients.
- Avoid the "SaaS cream" saturated default.
- No tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above sections.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over cleverness**: Interfaces should be immediately legible.
- **Data density**: Provide enough context without overwhelming the user.
- **Trust through restraint**: Use color deliberately to highlight action, not for decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast for text.
- Reduced motion support.
- Clear focus states for keyboard navigation.
